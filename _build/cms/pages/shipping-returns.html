Shipping & Returns

Fast, flat and simple. And if a paddle doesn't suit your game, send it back.

This is a demo store: nothing is shipped and no payment is taken. The policies below show how a real store page would read.

Shipping

MethodDeliveryCost
Standard (flat rate)3 to 5 business days$6.95, free over $75
Express1 to 2 business days$18.00
Nets and rebounders5 to 7 business daysFree

Orders placed before 1 pm Pacific on a business day leave the workshop the same day. You will get a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel is scanned.

The 60-day play test

Play any paddle for up to 60 days. If it isn't right, return it in any condition short of damage from misuse and we will refund the full price or exchange it for another model.

Returns

  • Unused gear can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.
  • Balls can be returned unopened within 30 days.
  • Return shipping is free for exchanges and $6.95 for refunds.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving.

Warranty

Every Third Shot paddle carries a two-year warranty against core crush, delamination and edge guard failure under normal play.
